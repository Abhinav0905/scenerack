# SceneRack: judging and delivery checklist

Problem: a small production can approve two costumes that need the same physical piece. Why solve it: the conflict forces a rental or a rushed design change. Approach: YouCam appearance review feeds a constrained wardrobe allocation. Result: an actionable pull/borrow/rent list, with a reversible $65-to-$0 sample decision.

The official four criteria are equally weighted. This checklist maps the working evidence to each criterion; it does not predict a score or a prize.

| Criterion | What the judge can inspect | Remaining evidence gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technological implementation | Real server-to-server YouCam tasks, durable results, approval invalidation, constrained allocation, private photo checks, 41 automated checks | Mobile interaction has not been tested; live generation has a finite unit allowance. |
| Design | Problem-first landing page, before/after photos, scene board, visible cost changes, separate rejection and preview-quality actions, export | A professional costume lead has not tested the interaction. |
| Potential impact | A named user and a concrete sourcing problem; $65 modeled scenario difference; the pull list is an operational output | No interviews, observed real savings, adoption, revenue or customer validation. |
| Quality of idea | A visual preference changes a shared-stock decision across performers and scenes | No claim of worldwide novelty; existing theatre tools already handle inventories and plots. |
