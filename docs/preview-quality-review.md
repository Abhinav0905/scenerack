**SceneRack: visual review of 12 recorded YouCam previews**

Reviewed October 31, 2026, Pacific time, after all 12 entries in `.sites-runtime/render-results.json` reported `success`. The corresponding completion timestamps use UTC on November 1. All result images are 768 × 1024 pixels.

This was an AI-assisted visual inspection with `view_image`, against each matching `public/assets/actor-{name}.jpg` and `public/assets/garment-{color}.jpg`. It was not a review by a costume professional. The input people and garments are synthetic. No output was edited, regenerated or retouched during this review.

"Useful" means the image supports a limited discussion of the garment's broad color, styling and silhouette on the fictional performer, with the original garment visible beside it. It does not mean exact product fidelity, physical fit, comfortable movement or a safe changeover. Face judgments below concern visual resemblance to the synthetic source, not biometric verification. Hand judgments concern obvious visible artifacts, not a detailed anatomy test.

**Result: 11 useful for this limited appearance review; 1 should be flagged as an inaccurate render.** This is a small, selected fixture set, not an accuracy estimate for the API. API task completion and appearance usefulness are separate outcomes.

The clearest main walkthrough pair is Leo/navy plus Maya/brown. Leo/burgundy and Imani/charcoal also support a readable comparison of distinct garment types. The olive previews should carry the trim caveat if used in close-up. The ivory result should be presented as replacing a shirt, not preserving one under an outer layer.

For Leo/brown, use the application's inaccurate-preview action and retain the original garment as an eligible physical item. A new result or a real fitting could resolve the issue later. This review did not make either happen.

No gross face substitution, extra person, missing hand or obviously duplicated hand was observed in these 12 frames at their displayed resolution. Small detail differences remain visible. All 11 outerwear outputs retain the gray shirt in visible areas, while the one shirt-replacement output replaces it. Those observations are limited to the inspected images.
